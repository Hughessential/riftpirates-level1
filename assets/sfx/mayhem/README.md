# Mayhem weapon sound bank

Eighteen distinct WAV samples were selected from the user-supplied
`Video Game Sound FX.zip`, specifically **The Essential Retro Video Game Sound
Effects Collection [512 sounds] by Juhani Junkala**. Its included INFO states
that these sounds are released under CC0. The original INFO is retained as
`SOURCE-INFO.txt`; `sources.json` maps each runtime cue to its archive path.

Original sample bytes are preserved. The runtime adds short gain envelopes,
per-weapon cooldowns, an 18-voice budget and SFX compression, keeping dense
weapon combinations audible without swallowing battle warnings or music.
